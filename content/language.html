Comparison: generating feedback from evidence or directly from video

AIFit generates messages using a fixed grammar after detecting geometric deviations. FormCoach: Lift Smarter, Not Harder asks vision-language models to compare user and expert videos and write corrections. Both connect the user to a reference, but the source of the message differs. [4, §4.4] [1, §2]

QuestionAIFitFormCoach
What generates the cue?An assessment table of measured deviations and a deterministic grammar.A prompted VLM given paired visual input and user preferences.

FormCoach: clear wording did not guarantee correct feedback

FormCoach evaluates 1,700 annotated video pairs across 22 exercises. In its automated evaluation, GPT-4.1 receives 58.2 accuracy, 94.4 actionability, and 74.26 hallucination in the paper's percentage columns. Actionability concerns the wording; hallucination means flagging a fault that is not present. These measures are not mutually exclusive categories: one response can contain something useful and also an unsupported correction. [1, §3; Tables 1–2]

The authors report weaker results for complex, multi-phase exercises than visually simple isolated movements. Viewpoint ambiguity and occluded joints make detailed comparison harder. This is a concrete weakness of video-based contextual coaching, despite its flexible language interface. [1, §4.1–4.2]

What can we conclude?

AIFit makes feedback generation inspectable; FormCoach allows more flexible wording but demonstrates unsupported advice. Their feedback scores cannot rank one against the other because their ground truths and metrics differ. Neither cited evaluation establishes that following a correction improves the next repetition.

SiTrEx provides another contrast: it reports per-angle correctness but does not yet give explicit instructions for correcting posture. Detecting a difference and explaining how to fix it remain separate capabilities. [5, conclusion]

Lesson for Project 2: Test the form detector separately from the wording of the cue. A language model should phrase supported evidence, with its output checked for unsupported additions. That is a recommendation from this review, not a validated solution to hallucination.
